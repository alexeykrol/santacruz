# Title: Perpetual Grace LTD Stars and Creators

## Steven Conrad Quotes

**On Mood and Genre:**
"When we get notes from the studio...we'll say, 'But it has to create a mood.' Otherwise, you need to figure out another way to do it."

**On Character Complexity:**
"When your show needs to become more complex, then maybe it's time for your characters to become more complex."

**On Casting and Ensemble Quality:**
"If the ensemble is not as strong as its strongest member, the hum of the show will be lesser than."

**On Text and Performance:**
"It isn't just the text, it's the text and the artist who knows that it's music, and that there ought to be a way to do that."

**On Genre Convention:**
"Noir is a stylized world...It helps to remind the audience what genre they're watching, so that they stay interested."

**On Television Standards:**
"It's not even a goal to be good anymore. It never comes up...now, in this landscape of television, it's almost a requirement that you try to do that."

**On Actor Capability:**
"He does not have limitations. He's still creating the spectrum."
